Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Overview

We help organizations make better decisions by listening to the people they serve. Our team designs and conducts rigorous survey research and opinion polling that gives leaders a clear picture of community needs, priorities, and satisfaction. Whether you're evaluating a new initiative, gauging public sentiment, measuring service quality, or assessing workforce morale, we deliver the reliable data you need to act with confidence.

From questionnaire design through final reporting, we manage every phase of the research process.

What We Offer

Community Surveys

We design and field surveys that capture how residents experience and prioritize the services, infrastructure, and programs their community provides. Our community surveys are built to reach a representative cross-section of the population, not just the most vocal residents, so findings reflect the full range of perspectives rather than a self-selected sample.

Opinion Polling

When organizations need to understand public sentiment on a specific issue, proposed policy, or pending decision, we conduct targeted opinion polls with the methodological rigor of professional research. We pay careful attention to question wording, order effects, and response scales to ensure results reflect genuine opinion rather than artifacts of poor survey design.

Resident Satisfaction Surveys

Satisfaction surveys give organizations an ongoing measure of how well they are meeting resident expectations. We design instruments that produce actionable scores across service areas, track change over time, and identify the specific drivers of satisfaction and dissatisfaction so that improvement efforts can be focused where they will have the most impact.

Workforce Surveys

Understanding employee experience is essential to organizational health. We conduct workforce surveys that assess engagement, morale, and workplace culture with the same rigor we bring to community research. Our workforce surveys are designed to encourage candid responses, and our reporting helps leadership interpret findings in the context of peer organizations and best practices.

Our Approach

We build each project from the ground up, starting with your questions and working backward to the methodology that will answer them most effectively.

Representative Sampling

We build sampling frameworks that reflect your population's full demographic and geographic makeup. By deploying across online, phone, and in-person channels, we connect with people who are often underrepresented in standard data collection, not just those who are easiest to reach.

Rigorous Analysis

We analyze results with statistical rigor, applying appropriate weighting and significance testing. Our reports distinguish between findings that are robust and those that should be interpreted with caution.

Actionable Reporting

We present findings in formats built for action: clear topline summaries for leadership, detailed crosstabs for analysts, and presentation-ready visualizations for boards and public meetings.
