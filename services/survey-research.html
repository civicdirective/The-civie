Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Overview

We help local governments make better decisions by listening to the people they serve. Our team designs and conducts rigorous survey research and opinion polling that gives municipal leaders, county administrators, and public agencies a clear picture of community needs, priorities, and satisfaction. Whether you're evaluating a new transit initiative, testing support for a proposed bond measure, gauging resident sentiment on public safety, or planning for long-term growth, we deliver the reliable data you need to act with confidence.

From questionnaire design through final reporting, we manage every phase of the research process.

Our Approach

We build each project from the ground up, starting with policy questions and working backward to the methodology that will answer them most effectively.

Survey and Poll Design

We develop questionnaires that capture genuine opinion rather than manufacturing it. That means careful attention to question order, wording, and response scales—reducing acquiescence bias, social desirability effects, and other pitfalls that undermine credibility.

Representative Sampling

We build sampling frameworks that reflect your jurisdiction's full demographic and geographic makeup. By deploying across online, phone, and in-person channels, we connect with residents who are often underrepresented in public input processes—not just those who show up to meetings or follow local government on social media.

Rigorous Analysis

We analyze results with statistical rigor, applying appropriate weighting and significance testing. Our reports distinguish between findings that are robust and those that should be interpreted with caution.

Actionable Reporting

We present findings in formats built for action: clear topline summaries for officials, detailed crosstabs for planners, and presentation-ready visualizations for public meetings.
