Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Overview

We help local governments make better decisions by listening to the people they serve. Our team designs and conducts rigorous survey research that gives municipal leaders, county administrators, and public agencies a clear picture of community needs, priorities, and satisfaction. Whether you're evaluating a new transit initiative, gauging resident sentiment on public safety, or planning for long-term growth, we deliver the reliable data you need to act with confidence.

From questionnaire design through final reporting, we manage every phase of the research process.

Our Approach

We build each project from the ground up, starting with policy questions and working backward to the methodology that will answer them most effectively.

  • Survey instrument design and validation
  • Sampling methodology and strategy
  • Multi-modal deployment (online, phone, in-person)
  • Statistical analysis and reporting
  • Data visualization and presentation
