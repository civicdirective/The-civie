Urban Planning

Comprehensive planning support that helps communities grow thoughtfully, equitably, and in alignment with their long-term vision.

Overview

Good planning shapes communities for generations. Our urban planning services help local governments navigate the complexities of growth, land use, and development while staying true to the values and priorities of the people they serve. Whether you're updating a comprehensive plan, managing development pressure, or reimagining an underutilized corridor, we provide the analytical foundation and process support to move forward with confidence.

We approach planning as both a technical discipline and a civic exercise. The best plans emerge from rigorous analysis combined with meaningful public engagement, and they succeed when they translate vision into implementable action.

Our Approach

Comprehensive and Long-Range Planning

We help communities articulate where they want to be in 10, 20, or 30 years, and chart a course to get there. Our long-range planning work integrates land use, transportation, housing, economic development, and environmental considerations into a coherent framework. We ground visioning exercises in demographic and market realities so that goals are ambitious but achievable.

Development Review and Zoning Analysis

We support planning departments with technical review of proposed developments, helping to assess consistency with adopted plans, evaluate traffic and infrastructure impacts, and identify conditions that protect community interests. When zoning codes need updating, we analyze existing regulations, benchmark against peer communities, and draft amendments that advance policy goals while remaining legally defensible.

Corridor and District Planning

Focused area plans allow communities to apply detailed attention to places undergoing change, whether a commercial corridor in need of revitalization, a neighborhood facing development pressure, or a downtown seeking to strengthen its identity. We facilitate planning processes that engage stakeholders, analyze existing conditions, and produce implementable recommendations.

Housing and Land Use Analysis

Understanding housing markets is essential to effective planning. We analyze housing supply, affordability, and demand trends to inform land use decisions. Our work helps communities understand the relationship between zoning, housing production, and affordability, and identify policy levers that can expand housing choice.

Public Engagement and Process Design

Planning legitimacy depends on inclusive participation. We design engagement strategies that reach beyond the usual voices, using surveys, workshops, online tools, and targeted outreach to ensure that plans reflect the full diversity of community perspectives. We help you move from input to synthesis, translating what you hear into actionable direction.
