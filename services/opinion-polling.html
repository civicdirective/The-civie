Opinion Polling

Rigorous public opinion research that provides actionable insights into constituent priorities and preferences.

Overview

Public opinion shapes public policy, but only when it's measured well. Our opinion polling practice gives local governments a reliable read on what constituents actually think, want, and expect from the institutions that serve them. We design and field polls that cut through anecdotes and assumptions to reveal where your community stands on the issues that matter most.

Whether you're testing support for a proposed bond measure, tracking satisfaction with city services over time, or trying to understand how residents prioritize competing budget needs, our polling delivers findings you can trust and act on. The result is a clearer line between what your community needs and the decisions you make on their behalf.

Our Approach

Good polling starts before the first question is asked. We treat every engagement as a custom research project, not an off-the-shelf product.

Defining What You Need to Know

We begin by working closely with your team to identify the specific decisions our research needs to inform. Getting this right up front keeps the project focused and the results useful.

Designing for Accuracy

We develop questionnaires that capture genuine opinion rather than manufacturing it. That means careful attention to question order, wording, and response scales. We strive to reduce acquiescence bias, social desirability effects, and the other pitfalls that undermine poll credibility.

Reaching a Representative Cross-Section

We build sampling frameworks that reflect your jurisdiction's full demographic and geographic makeup. By deploying across online, phone, and in-person channels, we connect with residents who are often underrepresented in public input processes — not just those who show up to meetings or follow local government on social media.

Turning Data into Direction

We analyze results with statistical rigor, then present findings in formats built for action. That means clear topline summaries for officials and presentation-ready visualizations for public meetings.
