Usability Studies

Rigorous user experience research that helps organizations design services people actually use and trust.

Overview

When users struggle to navigate a portal, abandon an online form, or misunderstand key information, the cost falls on everyone: staff time increases, service delivery slows, and trust erodes. Usability studies reveal exactly where those friction points are and why they exist, so you can fix them before they become entrenched problems.

We conduct systematic research with real users to evaluate digital tools, public-facing documents, in-person service workflows, and online portals. Our findings are grounded in behavioral evidence, not assumptions, and our recommendations are practical and prioritized for your capacity and budget.

Our Approach

Defining Research Questions

Every usability study begins with a clear problem statement. Are users completing online applications at lower rates than expected? Are support volumes high for a process that should be self-service? We work with your team to identify the specific interactions and outcomes that matter most, ensuring the research produces findings you can act on rather than a generic audit.

Recruiting Representative Participants

Meaningful usability research requires participants who reflect your actual service population, not just tech-savvy volunteers. We design recruitment strategies that capture the range of digital literacy levels, language backgrounds, and accessibility needs present in your community. This is where our survey research expertise adds direct value: we know how to reach and engage diverse populations.

Conducting Moderated and Unmoderated Testing

We employ a range of testing methods matched to your research questions and timeline. Moderated think-aloud sessions surface the reasoning behind user behavior and reveal confusion that analytics alone cannot detect. Unmoderated remote tests allow for larger samples and natural task completion in realistic environments. We design scenarios that reflect genuine service interactions, not artificial lab conditions.

Analyzing Behavioral Evidence

We synthesize session recordings, task completion rates, error patterns, and participant feedback into coherent findings. Our analysis distinguishes between surface-level preferences and substantive usability barriers: the kinds of problems that prevent users from successfully completing what they came to do. We apply established heuristics while keeping your specific context and constraints in focus.

Delivering Actionable Recommendations

Our reports are written for implementation, not for filing. Each finding is tied to a specific, prioritized recommendation with enough detail for your development team, communications staff, or service designers to act without ambiguity. We also offer facilitated debriefs to walk stakeholders through the findings and build internal consensus around next steps.

Supporting Iterative Improvement

Usability is not a one-time fix. We can help you establish lightweight testing practices your team can sustain between formal studies, track improvement over time, and build a culture of evidence-based service design. Our goal is to leave your organization better equipped to learn from users continuously, not just when a consultant is in the room.
