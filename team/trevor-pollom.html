Trevor Pollom, PhD

Principal Analyst

Trevor Pollom is a respected social scientist and survey researcher, with over 10 years of experience advising leadership figures in government, academia, and private industry. At Civic Directive, Trevor is the Principal and Technical Lead for most polling and survey related projects, including public opinion and employee satisfaction initiatives.

Pollom started his career in academia, leading National Science Foundation funded research in the US and Tanzania. Trevor's initial academic research focused on an Indigenous group of hunter-gatherers (the Hadza) in East Africa. He went on to support National Institute of Health projects, surveying veterans and Native American populations in the United States. Trevor's research from this period has been published in academic journals, including The American Journal of Human Biology, Human Nature, and Nursing Practice.

Trevor's domestic policy research has influenced elected government leaders and has been used to assess state-wide programs. Working in conjunction with the Nevada Institute for Children's Research and Policy, Trevor has presented policy briefs and analyses to the Nevada State Legislature on topics related to opioids, childcare access, and educational outcomes.

Pollom also spent several years as a researcher at Google, where he used survey and interview data to help leaders make effective strategy decisions. Trevor's research led to development and improvement of the features and products available on Android devices.
