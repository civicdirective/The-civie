About Us

Civic Directive transforms data into actionable insights and decision frameworks.

Our team is composed of seasoned professionals with decades of combined experience across the public, private, and nonprofit sectors. We understand the challenges that organizations face and bring enterprise-level analytical capabilities to clients of every size.

We believe every organization deserves access to rigorous, unbiased research that can guide critical decisions. Our commitment to honesty, methodological rigor, and measurable impact drives everything we do.

Our Services

Civic Directive provides community leaders with advanced analytics.

Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Usability Studies

User experience research that identifies friction in government services and delivers clear, prioritized improvements.

Public Engagement

Participation processes that connect organizations with the people they serve, turning community voice into evidence for better decisions.

Demand Forecasting

Rigorous projections of public service demand to support resource planning, infrastructure investment, and fiscal decision-making.

Program Evaluation

Rigorous evaluation of whether programs are working, with outcomes frameworks and data systems analysis to drive improvement.

Policy Analysis

Evidence-based evaluations of public policies to assess effectiveness, impacts, and alternatives.
