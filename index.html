About Us

Civic Directive is a premier analytics firm dedicated to serving the unique needs of local governments.

Our team is composed of seasoned professionals with decades of combined experience working for local governments and leading private companies, including major technology firms. Our Services

Civic Directive employs a team of analysts with PhDs, specializing in survey research, opinion polling, and economic analytics.

Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Opinion Polling

Rigorous public opinion research that provides actionable insights into constituent priorities and preferences.

Economic Impact Analysis

Data-driven economic modeling to understand how policies, projects, and investments affect your local economy.

Economic Forecasting

Forward-looking projections that help governments plan budgets, anticipate trends, and prepare for the future.

Feasibility Studies

Thorough assessments to determine the viability of proposed initiatives, developments, and policy changes.

Policy Analysis

Evidence-based evaluations of public policies to assess effectiveness, impacts, and alternatives.
