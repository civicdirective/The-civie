About Us

Civic Directive is a premier analytics firm dedicated to serving the unique needs of local governments.

Our team is composed of seasoned professionals with decades of combined experience working in local government, Fortune 500 companies, and academia. We understand the challenges that small governments face and bring enterprise-level analytical capabilities to communities of every size.

We believe that every community deserves access to rigorous, unbiased research that can guide critical policy decisions. Our commitment to honesty, methodological rigor, and measurable impact drives everything we do.

Our Services

Civic Directive employs a team of expert analysts specializing in survey research, spatial analysis, and economic analytics.

Survey Research

Comprehensive survey design, deployment, and analysis to capture the voice of your community with statistical precision.

Geographic Information Systems

Spatial analysis and mapping that transforms location data into actionable insights for planning and operations.

Urban Planning

Comprehensive planning support for development review, long-range visioning, and community growth strategies.

Economic Forecasting

Forward-looking projections that help governments plan budgets, anticipate trends, and prepare for the future.

Feasibility Studies

Thorough assessments to determine the viability of proposed initiatives, developments, and policy changes.

Policy Analysis

Evidence-based evaluations of public policies to assess effectiveness, impacts, and alternatives.
