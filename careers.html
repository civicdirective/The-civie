Careers

Join our team of analysts dedicated to empowering local governments with data-driven insights.

Current Openings

We do not have any open positions at this time.

Civic Directive periodically seeks analysts to join our team. We are especially interested in candidates with backgrounds in usability research, human-computer interaction, or user experience design, though we value a range of analytical backgrounds including survey research, economic analysis, urban planning, and policy evaluation.

Why Work With Us

At Civic Directive, you will work on meaningful projects that directly impact communities. Our team collaborates with local governments across the country, helping them make better decisions through rigorous analysis and clear communication.

We value intellectual curiosity, methodological rigor, and a commitment to public service. If this sounds like you, we encourage you to check back for future opportunities.

Stay in Touch

Interested in future opportunities? Send your resume and a brief introduction to contact@civicdirective.com. We will keep your information on file and reach out when positions become available.
